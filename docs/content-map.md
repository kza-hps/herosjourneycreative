# Hero’s Journey Creative — Content Map

This document maps all routes and their intended purposes in the structural scaffold.

The current repo UI is only a scaffold. Do not treat the current layout, spacing, colours, or components as final design. Main source materials for the design pass are `/public/brand` and `docs/design-grammar.md`.

## Route Architecture

| Route | Page | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `/` | **Home** | The front entry point displaying the primary brand tagline, Kiwi location details, four focus area lanes, and contact CTAs. |
| `/about` | **About** | Explains the studio's core philosophy (narrative as human infrastructure) and its hybrid focus on analog writing and digital AI experiments. |
| `/workshops` | **Workshops** | Showcases standard writing structures (morning journals, mythic world-building cohorts) for community and corporate teams. |
| `/legacy-writing` | **Legacy Writing** | Describes bespoke archival biography creation, memoirs, and digital porting for private commissions. |
| `/personal-myth-authoring` | **Personal Myth Authoring** | Details the Campbellian hero's journey consultation framework mapping biography to narrative structures. |
| `/journal` | **Journal** | Lists a series of static essays/notes on language, workshops, and AI collaborations. |
| `/showcase` | **Showcase** | Displays a portfolio grid of case studies spanning memoirs, interactive portals, and community programs. |
| `/contact` | **Contact** | Provides a central contact intake form and location details for prospective clients and collaborators. |

## Placeholder content warning

Some scaffold content is intentionally provisional. Do not treat dates, phone numbers, case studies, cohorts, project names, service claims, or contact details as confirmed business facts unless explicitly supplied by the site owner. Placeholder content may be inaccurate.
